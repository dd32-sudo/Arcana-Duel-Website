ARCANA DUEL

Where Fate Is Your Weapon

HOME FEEDBACK
Long ago, the 22 Major Arcana were scattered across the mortal world. Two duelists gather, each holding six random cards from the same ancient deck. Through strategy, timing, and a touch of luck, they clash until one falls. The winner earns a gift from the cosmos — a single question answered by the tarot.

⚔️ Battle Cards (7 Types)

  • The Sun — Deal 2 damage (strongest attack)
  • Justice — Deal 1 damage (reliable strike)
  • The Chariot — Gain 1 shield (blocks next hit)
  • The Tower — Cancel opponent's card effect
  • Wheel of Fortune — Steal a random card from enemy
  • The High Priestess — Opponent skips next turn
  • Strength — Restore 2 HP (only healing card)

🎮 How to Play

Pass-and-play — Two players share one screen. A cover screen hides the hand between turns.

Rounds — Each round, first player picks up to 2 cards. Second player sees what was played and responds.

Win Conditions — Reduce enemy HP to 0, or have more HP when cards run out.

Special Combo — Play The Sun + Wheel of Fortune together once per game to restore 5 HP instead of normal effects.
